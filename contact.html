Contact and Support

Read the booking process and rules, or contact the administrator.

Booking Process

  1. Check room availability on the Rooms page
  2. Fill out the form on the Booking page
  3. Wait for confirmation from the administrator
  4. Use the room according to the Schedule page
  5. Return the room in a tidy condition

Booking Rules

  • Book at least one day in advance
  • One booking for one room and one session
  • Number of participants must not exceed capacity
  • Heavy food is not allowed
  • Report facility damage to the administrator
